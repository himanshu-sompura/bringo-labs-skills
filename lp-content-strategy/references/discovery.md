# Landing page discovery

Ask only what is missing. Use up to three short questions at a time.

| Topic | Ask when missing |
| --- | --- |
| Buyer | Who is it for? Why now? What result do they want? |
| Traffic | Where did they come from? What did they search for or expect? |
| Offer | What do they get, how does it work, what does it cost, and who qualifies? What would they do instead? Why choose this? |
| Trust | What might stop them? What proof answers that doubt? |
| Action | What should they do? What happens next? What must they know first? |

Use buyers' words from calls, reviews, or messages. Note what they already know about the problem or offer. Keep buyer quotes, user claims, verified facts, and assumptions distinct. Preserve approved wording and section order.

Draft once the buyer, entry promise, offer, and action are clear. Use the strongest claim the evidence supports. Narrow or drop claims that lack proof. Flag gaps only if they affect the copy.
