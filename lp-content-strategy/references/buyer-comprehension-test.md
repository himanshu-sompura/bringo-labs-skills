# Buyer comprehension test

Use with likely buyers when the offer, fit, or CTA could be misread. Write down the expected answers first. Use the likely device for a rendered page. With copy alone, assess wording and order. A teammate’s feedback is an editorial review.

1. **First screen.** Show the real ad or outreach first when applicable. Ask “What’s offered?”, “Who is it for and when?”, and “What happens if you click?” Ask what gave them that impression.
2. **Whole page.** Give a realistic buyer goal without naming the CTA. Watch their first action and let them reach the next screen unaided. Stop before live submission unless it’s part of the test.

Record who tested what, their words, first action, and hesitation. Compare with the approved offer and actual flow.

| Misread | First fix |
| --- | --- |
| Wrong offer or audience | Clarify the first screen. |
| Wrong responsibilities | Name who does the work near the offer. |
| Wrong next step | Clarify the button and nearby copy. |
| Missed key term | Move it beside the decision it affects. |
| Couldn’t find the action | Check the rendered layout. |

Fix material misreads and retest with a fresh buyer. Use live conversion data to judge performance.
